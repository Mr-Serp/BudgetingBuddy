How to read this

Utilization is your reported revolving balance divided by your credit limit. It’s one factor in credit scores, alongside payment history, credit age, credit mix, and new credit.

The calculator shows utilization. A bureau score requires credit report data and a scoring model.

After-payment figures assume no new charges, interest, or fees, and that the lower balance is reported.
